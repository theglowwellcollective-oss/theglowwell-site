The 3-Step Routine That Cleared My Hormonal Acne

If you’re struggling with hormonal acne, I totally get it. I was right there with you, battling breakouts like they were my ex who just wouldn’t leave. But I finally found a hormonal acne 3-step routine that actually works for me, and I’m excited to share it! This routine is simple but effective. It cleared up my skin and helped me feel more confident.

How I Cleanse My Skin

First, I start with a gentle cleanser. You want something that cleans without stripping your skin. Look for sulfate-free options. I usually go for something with salicylic acid in it since it helps to keep pores clear. I wash my face twice a day—morning and night. And don't forget to use lukewarm water! Hot water can actually irritate your skin.

How I Treat Hormonal Breakouts

Next up is treatment. This part is so important! I use a treatment with benzoyl peroxide for spot treatment on any stubborn bumps. You can also try a serum with niacinamide. It’s great for balancing oil production and calming inflammation. Just make sure you apply thin layers to avoid overdoing it. I've made that mistake before—trust me, too much product can backfire!

How I Moisturize

Lastly, I can’t skip moisturizer. Even if your skin is oily, it still needs hydration. Look for a lightweight, oil-free moisturizer. Some people think moisturizing can make hormonal acne worse, but it actually helps in the long run. I like to use one with hyaluronic acid because it’s super hydrating without being heavy.

My 3-Step Routine Checklist

  • Gentle Cleanser: Look for a sulfate-free cleanser.
  • Treatment: Use benzoyl peroxide for spots or a niacinamide serum.
  • Moisturizer: Apply a lightweight, oil-free moisturizer.

Stick to this routine, and you might just see some amazing results like I did! Remember, consistency is key. It might take a little while, but it’s worth it.

FAQs About Hormonal Acne

What causes hormonal acne?

Hormonal acne is often caused by fluctuations in hormones, like during your menstrual cycle, pregnancy, or stress. It typically appears along the jawline and chin.

Can diet influence hormonal acne?

Yes! Certain foods can trigger inflammation. Eating healthy and staying hydrated can really help. I’ve noticed a difference when I cut back on sugar.

How long until I see results with this routine?

It can take a few weeks to notice improvement. Be patient and stick with your routine!
