What Your Skin Actually Needs in Winter vs Summer

Your skin has different needs in winter and summer, and adjusting your routine can save you from bad skin days! In winter, your skin gets dry and flaky, so you need heavy moisturizers and hydrating serums. But come summer, you’ll want lighter products that won’t clog your pores and maybe even some SPF action. I’ve learned the hard way — my obsession with skincare started when I tried to find a cure for my winter dryness and summer oiliness and wasted so much money on products that just didn’t work. Here’s the lowdown on what to do for seasonal skincare routines during winter and summer!

How I Changed My Routine with the Seasons

First things first, let’s look at how you can switch it up based on the season. Trust me, your skin will thank you.

Winter Skincare Essentials

  • Hydrating Cleanser: Choose something creamy and nourishing.
  • Rich Moisturizer: Think thick creams or oils to lock in moisture.
  • Humidifier: Pop one in your bedroom to keep air moisture levels up.
  • Extra Serums: Grab some hyaluronic acid or glycerin to layer under your moisturizer.

Summer Skincare Essentials

  • Gel Cleanser: Go for a lightweight formula to avoid feeling greasy.
  • Light Moisturizer: Think oil-free lotions or gels.
  • Sunscreen: SPF is a must, even on cloudy days!
  • Exfoliation: Increase your chemical exfoliation to avoid pores getting clogged.

Do You Need a Checklist for Your Routine?

You bet! Here’s a simple checklist, so you don’t miss anything, whether it’s winter or summer:

  • Identify your skin type: Is it dry, oily, or combo?
  • Assess the environment: Is it hot and humid or cold and dry?
  • Adjust your cleansers: Swap heavy for light or vice versa.
  • Update your moisturizers: From rich creams in winter to lightweight lotions in summer.
  • Incorporate serums: Tailor them to hydration in winter and oil control in summer.
  • Never forget sunscreen in summer!

Frequently Asked Questions

What if I have combination skin?

If you have combination skin, adjust different parts of your routine based on where you see dryness or oiliness. You don't have to go all-in for one season’s products!

When should I start switching my routine?

As temperatures start to change, start to swap! Usually, you'll know when it’s time based on how your skin feels. Trust me, it’ll talk to you.

Can I use the same products for both seasons?

You can, but it’s smart to tweak how you use them. Perhaps use a heavier serum in winter but apply it less frequently in summer to avoid feeling greasy.
