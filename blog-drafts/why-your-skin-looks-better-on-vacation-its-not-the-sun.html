Why Your Skin Looks Better on Vacation (It's Not the Sun)

Your skin looks better on vacation because you’re relaxed and often taking a break from daily stressors. You’re also likely drinking more water, eating fresh food, and maybe even getting a good dose of vitamin D. But it’s not just the sunshine, my friend! A change in routine does wonders for your skin. Trust me, I’ve seen it happen to my own skin after countless overpriced products didn't deliver. It’s refreshing, right?

How I Unplugged from Stress

  • Leave your work at home.
  • Practice mindfulness or meditation.
  • Engage in fun activities that make you laugh.
  • Spend time with loved ones.

How I Focused on Hydration

You know how it’s easy to get caught up in the hustle and forget to drink water? Well, on vacation, I’m usually near beaches or pools—lots of chances for hydration! Here’s how to keep your skin glowing:

  • Carry a water bottle everywhere.
  • Drink a glass of water before every meal.
  • Snack on fruits like watermelon or cucumbers.
  • Avoid too much alcohol; it can dehydrate your skin.

How I Changed Up My Skin Routine

  • Gentle cleanser.
  • Light moisturizer.
  • Sunscreen (Don't skip this, even if you think it’s just natural glow!).
  • Hydrating serum (if you have one you love!).

What I Ate Matters Too

  • Fill up on fruits and veggies.
  • Try to include healthy fats like avocados.
  • Enjoy local seafood or lean protein sources.

FAQs

  • Why does my skin look worse when I come home? Stress returns, climate changes, and often, I slip back into old eating habits.
  • Can I replicate vacation vibes at home? Definitely! Build self-care breaks into your day, drink more water, and eat fresh foods whenever possible.
  • What products should I use for post-vacation skin recovery? Stick with hydrators and gentle exfoliants to bring that glow back.
