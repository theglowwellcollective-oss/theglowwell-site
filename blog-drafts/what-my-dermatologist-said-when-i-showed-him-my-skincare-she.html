What My Dermatologist Said When I Showed Him My Skincare Shelf

When I finally showed my dermatologist my skincare shelf, he raised an eyebrow—more like four eyebrows, because I was definitely overdoing it. His main takeaway? Less is more. He couldn’t believe how many products I had, most of which were duplicates or didn’t even need to be there. The truth hurts, right? But hey, I was just trying to find the magic potion for my skin! If you’ve got a crowded shelf like I did, it’s time for a skincare shelf audit.

Why Skincare Shelf Audits Are Important

Trust me, having a cluttered shelf can actually mess with your skin. Products can expire or lose their effectiveness. Plus, layers of products can clog pores and irritate your skin. A good audit helps you focus on what works best for you. It’s like cleaning out your closet; you’ll feel so much better afterward!

How I Convinced Myself to Get a Dermatologist Review

Honestly, I was in denial. I thought my extensive collection was impressive. I mean, who doesn’t love a good haul? But after wasting a ton of money on products that didn’t work, I finally had to face the music. Scheduling that appointment was the best decision. My dermatologist was super kind but direct. He really helped me refocus my skincare approach, and now, my routine feels lighter, fresher, and way more effective.

What To Include in Your Skincare Shelf Audit

Ready to declutter your shelf? Here’s a simple checklist to help you out:

  • Gather all your products in one place.
  • Check expiration dates. Toss anything that's expired.
  • Identify duplicates. Do you really need five cleansers?
  • Evaluate active ingredients. Are they working for your skin type?
  • Decide what’s a must-have and what can go.
  • Keep a few versatile products for emergencies.
  • Organize by type — cleansers, treatments, moisturizers.

And if you’re feeling overwhelmed, make it a spa day with a friend! Trust me, it makes it way more fun.

FAQs About Skincare Shelf Audits

What if I’m not sure if a product works for me?

That's totally normal! Try using one new product at a time. Give it a few weeks before you decide if it’s a keeper or not. Your skin needs time to adjust.

How often should I do a skincare shelf audit?

I’d recommend doing it every 3-6 months. It’s a good way to keep tabs on what you’re actually using and what’s taking up space.

What should I do with the products I’m tossing?

You can recycle, donate, or give them to friends. Just make sure they’re clean and unopened if you decide to pass them on!
