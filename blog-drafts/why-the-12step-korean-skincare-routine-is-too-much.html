Why the 12-Step Korean Skincare Routine Is Too Much

Honestly? The Korean skincare 12-step routine is a bit over the top for most of us. It can feel like you're preparing for battle every time you wash your face! While I get the appeal — who doesn’t want glowing skin? — juggling all those steps can cause more stress than it’s worth. I’ve definitely been there, trying products that just left me frustrated and broke. You don’t need a million steps to take care of your skin, trust me!

But what are the main reasons it’s too much?

Let’s break it down. First, not everyone has the time or energy for a long routine every night. Busy schedules can easily push skincare down our priority list. Plus, too many products can irritate your skin. I remember layering on all those serums and waking up with a patchy, red mess. It's better to keep it simple, right?

How I cut down my skincare routine

After some trial and error, I finally nailed down a routine that works for me. Here’s how I simplified things:

  • Started with a gentle cleanser.
  • Used a toner that suits my skin type.
  • Picked one or two serums that target my skin's needs.
  • Applied a moisturizer that keeps my skin hydrated.
  • Don’t forget the sunscreen during the day!

That’s it! No need for 12 steps when these basics do the trick. It’s made a huge difference for me — more glow and less fuss.

What do I actually need for effective skincare?

If you're wondering what to prioritize, focus on these essentials:

  • A good cleanser.
  • A solid moisturizer.
  • Sunscreen (yes, EVERY day!).
  • Optional: a serum tailored to your skin type, like vitamin C for brightness.

With these, you can still achieve that dewy glow without overwhelming yourself.

FAQs

1. Can I get good results with a shorter routine?

Absolutely! You can still see great results by focusing on fewer, but effective products that work for your skin. Quality over quantity!

2. What if I love some steps from the 12-step routine?

That's okay! You can definitely mix and match steps that work for you. Find what feels good and stick to it!

3. How often should I change my products?

Give products at least a month to see how they work. But if something irritates your skin, toss it out earlier, girlfriend!
