How I Created My Budget-Friendly Skincare Routine

Building a skincare routine at Target doesn’t have to be overwhelming. Here’s how I do it:

  • Start with a gentle cleanser.
  • Use a hydrating serum.
  • Moisturize (don’t skip this!).
  • Apply sunscreen every morning.
  • Incorporate an exfoliating toner a couple of times a week.

That’s it! It doesn’t have to be complicated. I know it feels like every product claims to be the next big thing, but keeping it simple has really worked wonders for me. Just sticking to dermatologist-approved stuff has been my best decision yet!

What About Other Brands or Ingredients?

While I’m all about those Target staples, some other brands are great if you’re shopping online or at larger drugstores. Ingredients to look for include:

  • Hyaluronic Acid: For hydration.
  • Retinol: Great for anti-aging.
  • Niacinamide: Helps with redness and pores.

I’ve had some experience with trying too many trendy ingredients at once, and honestly, my skin had a meltdown! It's better to introduce new products one by one to see how your skin reacts.
