The Exact Order to Apply Skincare for Maximum Results

The order you apply your skincare really matters! If you want maximum results, start with the thinnest to thickest textures. So, cleanse first, then tone (if you use a toner), followed by serums, treatments, eye creams, and finally, moisture with your moisturizer or oil. Getting this order right can make a huge difference. I learned this the hard way, after years of slapping products on without a clue. Spoiler: my skin wasn’t too happy about it!

How I Organize My Skincare Routine

Here’s how I think about it. I’ve got a simple mental checklist, and you should totally borrow it! I like to remind myself that each step builds on the one before it.

  • Cleanser: Remove dirt and makeup.
  • Toner: Prep your skin. This helps the next products absorb better.
  • Serum: Target specific issues (like fine lines or uneven skin tone).
  • Treatments: This is where you tackle concerns—think retinol or acne treatments.
  • Eye Cream: Gentle taps to hydrate those delicate under-eye areas.
  • Moisturizer: Hydrate and lock all the goodness in!
  • Sunscreen: (Daytime only) Protect, protect, protect!

Have I Ever Messed This Up?

Oh, for sure! I once decided to skip a toner thinking it was unnecessary. I mean, how much difference could it make, right? Well, my serums didn’t seem to penetrate, and I ended up wasting a lot of good product. Don’t be like me—each step is there for a reason. Just think of it as building a house. You wouldn’t skip the foundation, right?

Can Different Skin Types Change This Order?

Great question! Yes, different skin types can tweak things a bit, but the general order still applies. If you have oily skin, you might want to skip heavy creams and stick to lightweight gels. If you’re super dry, you might layer on an extra moisturizer or oil. Just listen to your skin!

What Should I Do If My Skin Reacts Badly?

If you notice redness, bumps, or irritation, it’s time to reassess. Start by cutting out new products one by one. Test each for a week before adding anything back in. It’s kinda like dating, right? You want to see if you click before getting too serious.

What If I Don’t Have Time for All These Steps?

Honestly, I get it! Life is busy. Just prioritize! At the very least, always cleanse and moisturize. Adding in SPF during the day is a must too. If you want to go beyond that on a good skin day, then add in serums or treatments as needed.
