The Skincare Routine for When Your Skin Is Freaking Out

If your skin’s freaking out, take a deep breath and chill. First, you’ve gotta simplify your routine. Your skin's in crisis mode, so now is not the time to experiment with new products or layers of heavy makeup. Stick to gentle cleansers, soothing toners, and basic moisturizers. Trust me, cutting back will work wonders and help calm the chaos faster.

How I Deal with Skin Freaking Out

  • Step 1: Cleanse gently. Use a mild, fragrance-free cleanser.
  • Step 2: Tone it down. Choose a calming toner with ingredients like aloe or chamomile.
  • Step 3: Moisturize lightly. Go for a lightweight, non-comedogenic moisturizer.
  • Step 4: Soothe with serums. Look for calming serums with niacinamide or hyaluronic acid.
  • Step 5: SPF essential. Don’t skip your sunscreen, even on bad skin days!
  • Step 6: Hydrate from within. Drink plenty of water and eat skin-friendly foods.
