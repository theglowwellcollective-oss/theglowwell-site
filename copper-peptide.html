How to use it

One step. Morning and night.

Rich but not heavy. Designed to absorb cleanly without leaving a film, and layer with whatever you already use — not to add another routine on top.

Suggested use
  • 01 Cleanse with your usual face wash. Pat skin damp, not dry.
  • 02 Apply a pea-sized amount to face and neck — morning and night.
  • 03 Wait ~30 seconds, then layer SPF (AM) or your usual oil (PM) on top.
  • 04 Skip vitamin C in the same application — use it in alternate routines if you're already using it, not together.
