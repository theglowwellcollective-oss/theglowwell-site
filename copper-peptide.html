What's inside

Four actives. No fluff.

Each ingredient is here because the data — and decades of research — pointed back to it. Nothing for marketing optics.

Copper Tripeptide-1
GHK-Cu Copper Peptide
A naturally occurring tripeptide bound to copper. Plasma levels drop ~60% between age 20 and 60 — which is why it's the foundational active here. Supports the appearance of firmer, more resilient-looking skin.
Niacinamide (Vitamin B3)
Niacinamide
Long-studied B-vitamin form. Supports the look of a calmer, more even-toned skin barrier. Plays well with everything — including the peptide above.
Sodium Hyaluronate (multi-weight)
Hyaluronic Acid
Multi-weight molecules sit at different layers of skin instead of just the surface. Helps skin look hydrated, plump, and visibly bouncier.
Ceramide Complex
Ceramides
The lipid layer your skin naturally produces less of over time. Helps support the look of a stronger, more comfortable skin barrier.

How to use it

One step. Morning and night.

Rich but not heavy. Absorbs cleanly without leaving a film. Designed to layer with whatever you already use — not to add another routine on top.

  • 01 Cleanse with your usual face wash. Pat skin damp, not dry.
  • 02 Apply a pea-sized amount to face and neck — morning and night.
  • 03 Wait ~30 seconds, then layer your usual SPF (AM) or oil (PM) on top.
  • 04 Skip vitamin C in the same application — use it on alternate routines, not together.
