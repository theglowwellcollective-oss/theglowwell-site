Questions

A few things to know.

Does it contain fragrance?

Yes. It has a light honeysuckle scent. It contains no retinol and no dyes.

Is it OK for sensitive skin?

Copper peptides and fragrance can bother very reactive skin. Patch test on your inner arm first, start once a day, then build to twice.

Can I use it with vitamin C, retinol or acids?

Use strong acids and retinol at a different time of day. If in doubt, keep it simple: cleanser, serum, moisturizer, SPF.

What does 0.2% mean?

Copper Tripeptide-1 makes up 0.2% of the finished formula by weight. Our lab has confirmed this concentration in writing.
