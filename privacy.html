Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026 · theglowwell.com

The Glow Well is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

What we collect

Name and email address when you subscribe or purchase. Age, skin concern, and region when you complete the Glow Scan. We do not store photos — skin photos are analyzed in real time and immediately discarded.

How we use it

To deliver your skin analysis and personalized recommendations. To send emails about your routine, new products, and updates. We never sell your data to third parties.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity to understand how people use our site. These tools may use cookies.

Data deletion

To request deletion of your data, email us at hello@theglowwell.com. We will process all requests within 30 days.

GDPR rights for EU/UK users

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. Contact hello@theglowwell.com to exercise these rights.
